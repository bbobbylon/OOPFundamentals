AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner

The entry-level AWS certification (CLF-C02). A 41-question pool across all four exam domains — Cloud Concepts, Security & Compliance, Technology & Services, and Billing/Pricing/Support.

Foundational, broad, and very passable. The real CLF-C02 is 65 questions in 90 minutes at a 700/1000 scale (~70%). Use Practice mode to learn the service-to-purpose mappings (the bulk of the exam), then Exam mode for a timed 20-question dry run. Track your readiness on the dashboard.
