The Ping / ForgeRock Identity Platform

Ping Identity acquired ForgeRock in 2023; the product names converged onto a Ping prefix. A CIAM build rests on four engines — AM (access), IDM (lifecycle), DS (the store), and IG (the gateway) — running either self-managed or as the PingOne cloud. Knowing which box does what is half the job in an interview and on the job.

1. The four engines

🔐 PingAM — Access Management

The front door. Authentication journeys/trees, SSO sessions, OAuth2/OIDC & SAML, MFA, adaptive risk, and the policy engine (who can access what). Formerly ForgeRock AM / OpenAM.

🔄 PingIDM — Identity Management

The lifecycle engine. Registration & self-service, managed objects, connectors (AD, Salesforce, Workday), sync & reconciliation, workflow. Formerly ForgeRock IDM / OpenIDM.

🗄️ PingDS — Directory Services

The identity store — a hardened LDAP directory built for hundreds of millions of consumer identities with replication & low latency. Often the system of record. Formerly ForgeRock DS / OpenDJ.

🚪 PingGateway — Identity Gateway

The enforcement point. A reverse proxy that fronts apps/APIs, validates tokens, enforces AM policy, and retrofits SSO onto legacy apps that can't speak OIDC. Formerly ForgeRock IG / OpenIG.

Rule of thumb: AM decides who you are & what you may do, IDM manages the account over time, DS stores it, and IG enforces it at the edge.

3. Self-managed vs the PingOne cloud

Self-managed

You run AM/IDM/DS/IG on your own infra (VMs/k8s). Maximum control & customization; you own upgrades, scaling, HA. The classic ForgeRock stack.

PingOne (cloud)

SaaS CIAM — PingOne services (MFA, Risk, Verify, DaVinci) consumed via APIs. No infra to run. PingOne Advanced Identity Cloud is the managed ForgeRock stack as a service.

DaVinci (orchestration)

The cloud's no-code flow builder — the DaVinci equivalent of AM's journeys, wiring connectors (social, MFA, fraud, HTTP) into a login/registration flow.

6. Recap

🏗️ A Ping/ForgeRock CIAM platform = PingAM (authenticate, SSO, OAuth/OIDC, journeys, policy) + PingIDM (registration, lifecycle, provisioning, reconciliation) + PingDS (the high-scale identity store) + PingGateway (edge enforcement for legacy/APIs) — run self-managed or as PingOne cloud, with DaVinci as the cloud's journey builder. AM decides, IDM manages, DS stores, IG enforces.
