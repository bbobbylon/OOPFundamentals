AI / LLM Engineering — Practice Exam

Questions across transformers & attention, how LLMs actually work, prompt engineering, embeddings & vector databases, RAG, tool-calling agents, LLM API integration, fine-tuning vs RAG vs prompting & LLMOps, and AI safety & guardrails.

The mental model: an LLM is a next-token predictor wrapped in an application, and almost every practical question in this space is "which lever fixes this problem" — prompting, RAG, or fine-tuning — plus the security discipline of never letting untrusted content or an over-privileged tool masquerade as trusted instruction. Attention is what replaced RNNs' sequential bottleneck; embeddings are what let you search by meaning instead of keywords; RAG is what stops a model from confidently making things up about your own data; agents are what let a model take real actions, which is exactly why scoped credentials and prompt-injection defense matter as much here as anywhere else in application security.
