Docker — Practice Exam

A pool of questions across images & layers, the container lifecycle, Dockerfile authoring, volumes, networking, Compose, and production best practices.

The mental model: an image is a frozen template, a container is a running instance of it. Most Docker confusion comes from forgetting containers are ephemeral (data dies with them unless you mount a volume), and from Dockerfile layer caching (order your steps cheap-to-expensive). The classics on every test: RUN vs CMD vs ENTRYPOINT, COPY vs ADD, multi-stage builds, and why EXPOSE doesn’t actually publish a port. Practice mode links each answer to the visualizer that proves it. Drill until the bars are green on the dashboard.
