Git — Practice Exam

A 24-question pool across the three areas (working tree / staging / commits), branching & merging, rebase & history, remotes & collaboration, and undoing changes safely.

Almost every Git mistake comes from not knowing where your changes live. Working tree, staging area (index), and the commit history are three distinct places — and reset, checkout/restore, and revert each move data between them differently. The other classic: the golden rule of rebase (never rewrite history others have pulled). Practice mode links each answer to the visualizer that proves it. Drill until the bars are green on the dashboard.
