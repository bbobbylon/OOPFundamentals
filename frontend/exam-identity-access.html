Identity & Access — OAuth 2.0 · OIDC · JWT

The exam your CIAM day job actually tests. A 32-question pool across OAuth 2.0 grant types, OpenID Connect, JWT validation, sessions vs tokens, Spring resource servers, and how Ping & Entra fit in.

Authorization vs authentication is the whole game. OAuth 2.0 is delegated authorization (can this app act on my behalf?); OIDC layers authentication on top (who is this user?). The classic traps: using an access_token where you needed an id_token, forgetting to validate aud/iss/exp, skipping PKCE on a public client, and confusing a 401 with a 403. Practice mode links each answer to the visualizer that proves it. Drill until the bars are green on the dashboard.
