Kubernetes — Practice Exam

A pool of questions across core objects, Pods & controllers, Services & networking, ConfigMaps & Secrets, Deployments & scaling, and Helm.

Kubernetes is a declarative reconciliation loop: you describe desired state, controllers make reality match. The model that unlocks everything: a Deployment manages a ReplicaSet, which manages Pods; a Service gives those ever-changing Pods a stable address. The exam favorites: ClusterIP vs NodePort vs LoadBalancer, liveness vs readiness probes, requests vs limits, and that a Secret is only base64-encoded (not encrypted) by default. Practice mode links each answer to the visualizer that proves it. Drill until the bars are green on the dashboard.
