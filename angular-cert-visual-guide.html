1 Signals: propagation, laziness & memoization

What is this? A live dependency graph. qty and price are writable signals; total and tax are computed(); the effect logs. Why it matters: the exam loves two facts — computeds are lazy (writing a signal only marks dependents dirty; nothing recomputes until something reads them) and memoized (reading twice without changes = cache hit). Watch the dirty badges and the recompute counter prove both.

3 RxJS lab: the four flattening operators

What is this? Each "keystroke" starts a 2.4-second fake HTTP request. The four operators differ only in what they do when a new keystroke arrives while a request is in flight: switchMap cancels it, mergeMap runs them in parallel, concatMap queues, exhaustMap ignores the newcomer. Why it matters: "type-ahead search → which operator?" is a guaranteed question (answer: switchMap — stale requests get canceled). Mash the button and watch.

4 Router walk: first match wins, top-down

What is this? A real route table. Pick a URL and watch the router scan rows top-down — misses dim out, the first hit glows green. You'll see the empty-path redirect restart the walk, :id extract a param, a guard return a UrlTree and bounce you to /login, and ** catch everything at the bottom.
